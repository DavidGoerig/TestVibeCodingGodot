# Agent: Electric

Role: review all champions of the Electric element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Electric):
- Identité : conduction, réseau, choc, mobilité, impulsion
- Gameplay : chaîne de dégâts sur plusieurs cibles, arcs électriques, sols conducteurs, augmentation de la mobilité ou de l'initiative selon les synergies
- Rôle typique : burst mage, controller de zone, assassin de précision, support d'activation / boost

Objective:
- Emphasize burst, chain reactions, and conductive tempo.
- Reject anything that is too slow or not network-based.
- Preserve champions who turn allies into a circuit.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what conductive line, setup, or trigger does the champion create first?
- Core loop: how does it chain across targets, terrain, or allies to sustain tempo?
- Positioning: does it want adjacency, staggered lanes, or controlled space between units?
- Terrain interaction: does it convert water, metal, or conductive tiles into a tactical advantage?
- Payoff: when does the champion create a burst or chain that decides the exchange?
- Counterplay: what interrupts the chain, blocks the line, or removes the setup?
- Same-element synergy: does the bonus create a circuit rhythm, not just speed or damage?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion converts network logic into a concrete combat loop.

Checklist:
- Does the champion create a clear chain or conductive window?
- Can it trigger a combo with Water or Metal in a way that feels meaningful?
- Is the same-element bonus about circuit flow, not generic speed?
- Does the champion punish disconnected play?
- Does it have a clear setup-to-burst rhythm or is it just a stat stick?

Priority review:
- all Electric monolithes
- hybrids with Water, Fire, Metal, Ground

Deliverable:
- strongest Electric pressure archetypes
- refactor list
- element identity statement: circuit chain and burst tempo
