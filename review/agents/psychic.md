# Agent: Psychic

Role: review all champions of the Psychic element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Psychic):
- Identité : manipulation, contrôle d'entité, téléportation, espace, illusion
- Gameplay : téléporter, échanger, attirer, repousser, créer des portails / zones de téléportation, contrôler les lignes de vue et les positions
- Rôle typique : controller de combat, support de repositionnement, assassin de flanc, tank de bouclier / redirection

Objective:
- Keep champions that manipulate position, line breaks, and enemy timing.
- Remove units that just do random reposition without clear identity.
- Make Psychic champions feel like distortion logic, not generic support.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what mental or positional disruption does it create in the opening exchange?
- Core loop: what repeated manipulation of position, line, or timing defines its play?
- Positioning: does it want to break sight lines, swap units, or create weird angles?
- Terrain interaction: does it redirect or distort movement through portals, space control, or mental pressure?
- Payoff: when does the champion turn a small disruption into a decisive swing?
- Counterplay: what punishes its read of the board or forces it to fight in direct lines?
- Same-element synergy: does the team bonus create distortion and timing control, not a generic utility buff?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion consistently manipulates the battlefield and enemy decisions.

Checklist:
- Does the champion create weird angles, swaps, or displacements?
- Is the same-element bonus about pattern manipulation and control?
- Does the champion punish predictable movement?
- Does it have a clear weakness against blunt or rigid play?
- Is the real gameplay about timing disruption, not just teleporting on a cooldown?

Priority review:
- all Psychic monolithes
- hybrid review with Flying, Shadow, Metal, Ground

Deliverable:
- strongest positional reversal archetypes
- weak spell kits to rework
- identity: space distortion and enemy timing disruption
