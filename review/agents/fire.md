# Agent: Fire

Role: review all champions of the Fire element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Fire):
- Identité : ignition, pression, propagation, destruction, chaleur
- Gameplay : allumer les blocs / végétation / objets, créer fumée, cendre, chaleur, brûler les zones et rendre les sols dangereux
- Rôle typique : bruiser / zone damage, assassin à zone de feu, support de pression, tank incendiaire

Objective:
- Keep the champions who embody pressure, ignition, and tactical space control.
- Reject the archetypes that are just generic damage dealers.
- Make sure fire champions create a clear same-element rhythm: burn -> threaten -> collapse.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what ignition or threat window does the champion create at the start of a fight?
- Core loop: how does it establish fire pressure, then convert it into zone denial or burst?
- Positioning: does it want to stand in a lane, on the edge of cover, or at the center of a burn zone?
- Terrain interaction: does it light terrain, trigger burn chains, or force movement through heat?
- Payoff: when does the champion become explosive or oppressive?
- Counterplay: what extinguishes, blocks, or outranges it?
- Same-element synergy: does the team bonus create a fire tempo, or is it just raw damage?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion has a clear pressure rhythm and readable danger.

Checklist:
- Is there a real ignition sequence?
- Does the champion reshape space or force movement in a memorable way?
- Does the same-element bonus feel like a burning front, not a generic buff?
- Is the weakness clear and playable?
- Does the gameplay feel like pressure, not just “more fire damage”? 

Priority review:
- all 18 Fire monolithes
- all relevant Fire hybrids (especially Fire + Water, Fire + Electric, Fire + Ground)

Deliverable:
- 5 strongest Fire champions
- 3 to rework
- 1 to remove or replace
- clear statement: Fire identity = pressure front and explosive tempo
