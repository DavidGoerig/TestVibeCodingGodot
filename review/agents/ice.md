# Agent: Ice

Role: review all champions of the Ice element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Ice):
- Identité : solidification, géométrie, glisse, blocage, construction
- Gameplay : créer murs, ponts, rampes, plateaux, geler l'eau, immobiliser les unités, rendre les sols glissants
- Rôle typique : controller de parcours, assassin rapide / glissant, tank de fortification, artillery de glace

Objective:
- Keep champions who shape routes, freeze decisions, and create tactical geometry.
- Remove units that are just wall setters without pressure or mobility.
- Ensure Ice champions feel like battlefield architects.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: how does the champion start control over routes, elevation, or mobility?
- Core loop: what repeated action creates blocking, freezing, sliding, or geometry changes?
- Positioning: does it want to anchor a lane, force a detour, or create a trap on a narrow path?
- Terrain interaction: does it turn surfaces into walls, ramps, bridges, gliding zones, or frozen cover?
- Payoff: when does the champion create a decisive freeze or route denial?
- Counterplay: what breaks the freeze, melts the route, or punishes its stall tempo?
- Same-element synergy: does the team bonus create geometry logic and answerable movement, not just a wall buff?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion clearly modifies the board and creates a tactical decision.

Checklist:
- Does the champion reshape routes, bridges, walls, or cover?
- Is the same-element bonus about geometry and answerable movement?
- Does the champion feel threatening without being too slow?
- Is the weakness clear and exploitable by heat or melee pressure?
- Does it force a real decision over position, or does it just provide static defense?

Priority review:
- all Ice monolithes
- hybrid review with Water, Ground, Flying, Electric

Deliverable:
- strongest Ice route-shapers
- weak cases to replace
- identity: geometry control and tactical locking
