# Agent: Flying

Role: review all champions of the Flying element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Flying):
- Identité : altitude, vent, espace aérien, mobilité, attaque en hauteur
- Gameplay : survoler, contourner, se repositionner, utiliser le vent pour repousser / attirer, attaquer depuis la hauteur
- Rôle typique : assassin de profondeur, support de repositionnement, controller aérien, artillery de hauteur

Objective:
- Keep champions who feel like aerial repositioning, flanks, and vertical dominance.
- Avoid units that lack a clear angle or are too generic.
- Ensure flying champions always use height as a source of power.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: how does the champion establish vertical pressure or a flank angle from the first exchange?
- Core loop: what repeated movement pattern builds pressure from altitude over time?
- Positioning: does it want to circle, stay above the lane, or force enemies to rotate?
- Terrain interaction: does it manipulate wind, air lanes, height advantage, or anti-projectile angles?
- Payoff: when does the champion become decisive by repositioning or attacking from height?
- Counterplay: what punishes air dominance or forces it into grounded combat?
- Same-element synergy: does the bonus create aerial rhythm and flank pressure, not just extra mobility?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion turns height and repositioning into real tactical advantage.

Checklist:
- Does the champion gain a tactical advantage from altitude?
- Is the same-element bonus about air lanes and vertical repositioning?
- Does it create openings rather than only mobility?
- Is it vulnerable in a grounded, close-quarters fight?
- Is its positioning readable and exploitably punishable?

Priority review:
- all Flying monolithes
- hybrid review with Ground, Ice, Psychic, Shadow

Deliverable:
- strongest air-lane archetypes
- weak or duplicate cases
- identity: vertical reposition and flank control
