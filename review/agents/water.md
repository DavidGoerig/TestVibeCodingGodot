# Agent: Water

Role: review all champions of the Water element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Water):
- Identité : flux, mouvement, humidité, inondation, adaptation
- Gameplay : mouiller les cellules, ralentir, pousser, détourner, inonder des zones et créer des passages ou barrières fluides
- Rôle typique : support / heal / cleanse, controller de zones, assassin de mouvement, tank défensif de barrage

Objective:
- Preserve champions who feel like flow, route control, and team support.
- Remove champions that are only slow or too passive.
- Ensure Water champions make allies act through route creation and repositioning.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what route, field, or flow advantage does the champion secure early?
- Core loop: how does it maintain pressure through wet zones, route changes, and repositioning?
- Positioning: does it centralize around corridors, flood lanes, or cover rebuilds?
- Terrain interaction: does it create water, mud, flood, or fluid terrain that changes movement in a meaningful way?
- Payoff: when does the champion turn a controlled route into a decisive swing?
- Counterplay: what can break the flow, dry the route, or punish overextension?
- Same-element synergy: does the team bonus create continuity and support, not just healing?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion has a strong route-control rhythm and a readable reaction pattern.

Checklist:
- Does the champion manipulate routes or force movement?
- Does the same-element bonus create a support network, not just a buff?
- Is the Water identity about control and continuity, not generic healing?
- Does the champion have a strong counterplay?
- Does the champion feel fluid and reactive in real fights, or static and passive?

Priority review:
- all Water monolithes
- hybrid review with Fire, Electric, Ice, Grass

Deliverable:
- strongest Water archetypes
- weakest Water archetypes
- summary: Water = flux network + route control + reactive support
