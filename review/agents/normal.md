# Agent: Normal

Role: review all champions of the Normal element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Normal):
- Identité : adaptation, improvisation, neutralité, objets, terrain physique
- Gameplay : pousser / porter / lancer des objets, créer des barricades improvisées, utiliser le décor comme arme
- Rôle typique : tank polyvalent, support de repositionnement, assassin mobile, artillery improvisée

Objective:
- Keep the champions who turn terrain into a utility tool.
- Reject anything too generic or with no clear tactical identity.
- Make sure Normal champions feel like adaptors, not just neutral fillers.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what is the first meaningful move or setup that makes the champion matter?
- Core loop: what are the repeated actions that define its turn-to-turn play?
- Positioning: does it force a specific lane, elevation, object use, or route control?
- Terrain interaction: does it manipulate debris, barricades, boxes, cover, or improvised structures in a meaningful way?
- Payoff: what is the moment where it becomes decisive?
- Counterplay: what answer denies or punishes its plan?
- Same-element synergy: does the bonus support adaptation and team utility rather than just generic stat gain?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion has a clear tactical identity and a readable gameplay rhythm.

Checklist:
- Does the champion change the terrain in a useful way during combat?
- Does it have a distinct opening instead of just “good stat block”? 
- Can the player read its positioning and objective from the first seconds of a fight?
- Is the weakness specific and understandable?
- Is the same-element synergy about adaptation, rerouting, and enabling allies?

Priority review:
- read all 18 Normal monolithes
- identify the 4 strongest utility anchors
- identify the 2 weakest ones to rework or delete

Deliverable:
- list: keep / rework / remove
- summarize the element identity: "terrain utility and repositioning"
