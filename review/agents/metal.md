# Agent: Metal

Role: review all champions of the Metal element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Metal):
- Identité : structure, densité, inertie, précision, armure, rigidité
- Gameplay : fortifier les blocs et les structures, transformer les objets de terrain en barrières lourdes, projeter des masses / débris lourds
- Rôle typique : tank de structure, controleur de masse, artillery / lanceur de débris, support de fortification

Objective:
- Keep the champions who feel like fortified structure, impact, and counter-pressure.
- Reject generic heavy units without standout terrain identity.
- Make sure Metal champions feel anchored, dense, and punishing.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what wall, barrier, or mass pressure does it establish at the start of a fight?
- Core loop: how does it turn contact into locking, denying, or crushing opportunity?
- Positioning: does it prefer narrow lanes, anchor points, or bodyblocking space?
- Terrain interaction: does it fortify blocks, create barriers, or turn debris into impact tools?
- Payoff: when does the champion become decisive through collision or forced structure?
- Counterplay: what breaks the barrier, bypasses the mass, or punishes its rigid route?
- Same-element synergy: does the team bonus reinforce structure and counter-pressure, not just armor?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion creates meaningful lock and impact through mass and geometry.

Checklist:
- Does the champion reinforce structure or create crushing collisions?
- Is the same-element bonus about turning pressure into counter-pressure?
- Does it feel heavy and defensive without being deadweight?
- Is the weakness clear and exploitable?
- Does it produce a distinct combat rhythm, or is it just “tankier”? 

Priority review:
- all Metal monolithes
- hybrid review with Ground, Electric, Psychic, Fire

Deliverable:
- strongest fortress and impact archetypes
- low-impact units to replace
- identity: fortress mass and impact denial
