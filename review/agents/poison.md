# Agent: Poison

Role: review all champions of the Poison element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Poison):
- Identité : contamination, toxicité, empoisonnement, dégradation, piège
- Gameplay : créer du poison sur le sol / dans l'air, contaminer les objets et les blocs, appliquer un malus au fil du temps, créer des pièges et des nuages toxiques
- Rôle typique : trapper / controlleur, support anti-heal / debuff, assassin de seuil, tank toxique

Objective:
- Keep the champions who make the battlefield rot, poison, and punish attrition.
- Remove generic debuff units without an identity.
- Ensure Poison champions feel oppressive over time.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what poison setup or hazard does it establish before the real fight begins?
- Core loop: how does it force attrition, denial, or healing pressure over time?
- Positioning: does it want to stand on contaminated terrain, choke points, or slow routes?
- Terrain interaction: does it poison the board, create traps, spread hazards, or corrupt objects?
- Payoff: when does the poison become a decisive threat rather than a background debuff?
- Counterplay: what cleanses, removes, or outpaces the hazard?
- Same-element synergy: does the team bonus sustain attrition and denial, not just extra damage over time?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion turns contamination into a real tactical tempo.

Checklist:
- Does the champion create persistent danger or a corrupted zone?
- Is the same-element bonus about long-term attrition and denial?
- Does it have a strong anti-heal or anti-recovery identity?
- Does it have a good moment-to-moment tactical threat?
- Does it feel oppressive over multiple turns, not just a weak poison icon?

Priority review:
- all Poison monolithes
- hybrid review with Shadow, Ground, Grass, Psychic

Deliverable:
- best poison threat archetypes
- rework list
- identity: toxic setup and attrition denial
