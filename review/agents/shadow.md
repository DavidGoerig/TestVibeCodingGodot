# Agent: Shadow

Role: review all champions of the Shadow element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Shadow):
- Identité : obscurité, infiltration, dissimulation, corruption, furtivité
- Gameplay : devenir invisible dans les zones sombres, attaquer par le flanc ou la verticale, utiliser la pénombre pour contourner les défenses
- Rôle typique : assassin, disruptor / anti-vision, controller de flanc, support de confusion ou d'isolement

Objective:
- Keep the champions who feel like flankers, predators, and anti-vision assassins.
- Remove units that are just stealth copies without note-worthy play patterns.
- Make sure Shadow champions always conflict with vision and attention.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what angle, hidden route, or anti-vision setup does it exploit first?
- Core loop: how does it maintain pressure through surprise, flank, or timing disruption?
- Positioning: does it live in blind spots, behind cover, or on the vertical edge of the board?
- Terrain interaction: does it use darkness, cover, or negative space for ambush or path denial?
- Payoff: when does the champion become decisive through surprise and disruption?
- Counterplay: what reveals, punishes flanks, or forces it into direct duels?
- Same-element synergy: does the team bonus create anti-vision and infiltration rhythm, not just stealth?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion creates a clear ambush identity and a legitimate threat from the flank.

Checklist:
- Is the champion strong when breaking sight lines or positioning off the main route?
- Is the same-element bonus about blind-side pressure and disruption?
- Does the champion have a clear anti-vision identity rather than generic invisibility?
- Is the counterplay readable and satisfying?
- Does it feel like a predator, not just a stealth skin with good stats?

Priority review:
- all Shadow monolithes
- hybrid review with Poison, Psychic, Flying, Normal

Deliverable:
- strongest shadow predators
- rework list
- identity: blind-side pressure and infiltration control
