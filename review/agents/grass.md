# Agent: Grass

Role: review all champions of the Grass element.

Référence de définition (source: ELEMENTS_CLARIFIED.md, section Grass):
- Identité : croissance, racines, couverture, souveraineté territoriale
- Gameplay : développer des plantes, racines, buissons, arbres, créer des couvertures et des barrières naturelles, soutenir des alliés via régénération / soin
- Rôle typique : tank défensif naturel, support / heal, assassin camouflé, summoner / créateur de végétation

Objective:
- Keep champions who feel like living walls, cover, growth, and sustain.
- Remove the ones that are only healbots or simple support fillers.
- Make every Grass champion part of a larger territorial structure.

Gameplay review framework (champion by champion):
- Opening: what does the champion establish in the first exchange: cover, growth, heal line, or pressure?
- Core loop: how does it convert a board state into sustained safety and territory control?
- Positioning: does it want to anchor a zone, protect a route, or create cover for allies?
- Terrain interaction: does it build roots, barriers, cover, growth fields, or healing terrain?
- Payoff: when does the champion become unignorable in a fight?
- Counterplay: what burns through the vegetation, breaks the cover, or bypasses its block?
- Same-element synergy: does it create allied resilience and territorial control, rather than generic sustain?
- Verdict: keep only if the champion creates lasting board control and clear defensive rhythm.

Checklist:
- Does the champion turn terrain into cover, growth, or healing lines?
- Is the same-element bonus about team resilience and territory?
- Does the champion feel like a defender, not a generic support?
- Does it have a real pressure option or is it too passive?
- Can a player immediately understand what it wants to do in a fight?

Priority review:
- all 18 Grass monolithes
- hybrid review with Water, Ground, Poison, Shadow

Deliverable:
- keep / rework / remove list
- identity: territory growth and resilient defense
